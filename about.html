About Me

Research Interests

I primarily look at modelling complex fluids like asphalt and bio-based oils. So I'd say my interests are soft matter systems, model generation and molecular coarse-graining. I am extremely interested in advance sampling techniques within MD, including steered molecular dynamics, alchemical transformations and replica exchange. I am also currently developing my skills within DFT and machine learning.
